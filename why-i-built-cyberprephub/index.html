Why I Built CyberPrepHub

Cybersecurity is one of those fields where you are always learning something new. While studying for CISSP and working across different areas of security, I kept finding myself going back to the same concepts and looking for a simple explanation that actually made sense.

That is basically why I created CyberPrepHub.

I wanted one place where I could organise useful cybersecurity notes, explain concepts in plain language and keep building on what I learn over time. Some of the content will be focused on CISSP, but I also want to cover broader security and technology topics that are useful in day-to-day work.

This blog will be a more informal part of the site where I can write about cybersecurity, learning, certifications and things I come across along the way.

CyberPrepHub is still very new, but I’m looking forward to building it up gradually.
