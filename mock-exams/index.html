CISSP Mock Exams & Practice Tests

Practice questions are one of the best ways to identify gaps in your CISSP knowledge and become comfortable applying security concepts to scenario-based questions.

This page brings together reputable CISSP practice-question and mock-exam resources, including free, freemium and paid options.

🆓

Free Practice

Diagnostic tests, sample questions and limited free question banks.

TEST your knowledge
📝

Mock Exams

Longer timed exams help develop pacing, concentration and exam technique.

BUILD exam confidence
🎯

Target Weak Areas

Use results to identify which CISSP domains require more study.

FIND your gaps
Important

No practice test is the real CISSP exam

Practice questions should help you understand concepts, recognise knowledge gaps and improve your decision making.

They should not be treated as a way to memorise questions that you expect to encounter during the real CISSP examination.

Actual CISSP exam questions are confidential.

ISC2 prohibits candidates from copying, reproducing, distributing or discussing actual examination questions and answers.

Avoid websites claiming to provide genuine leaked CISSP exam questions, "brain dumps" or guaranteed copies of the real examination.

How to use practice questions properly

📚 Learn → Understand the CISSP concepts first
📝 Practice → Answer questions without checking notes
❌ Review → Understand WHY you were wrong
🔍 Identify → Find weak domains and concepts
📖 Relearn → Return to those topics
🧪 Retest → Check whether understanding improved

🔀 Why use more than one Question Bank? Avoid learning the questions instead of learning CISSP

Repeating the same question bank can eventually create misleadingly high scores.

Example

On your first attempt you score: 68%.

After repeating exactly the same test five times you score: 96%.

But have you learned the underlying concepts, or simply remembered which answer belonged to each question?

Fresh questions provide a better test of understanding

Using questions from different reputable authors helps test whether you can apply the concept when the wording and scenario change.

⏱️ How to take a Full Mock Exam Make the practice session realistic

A full mock exam can help test more than cybersecurity knowledge.

It also tests concentration, pacing and decision making over a longer period.

Use a timer

Practise making decisions without unlimited time.

Do not use notes

Treat the mock as an assessment rather than another study session.

Avoid interruptions

Create a realistic period of sustained concentration.

Answer every question seriously

Do not abandon difficult questions simply because the result is only practice.

Review afterwards

The review is often more educational than the score itself.

🤖 Mock Exams vs the Real CISSP CAT Exam Practice simulations have limitations

The real CISSP examination uses Computerized Adaptive Testing.

CAT continuously estimates the candidate's ability and uses previous answers when selecting subsequent questions.

A conventional fixed mock test cannot reproduce this process exactly.

Mock Exam

Usually selects a predefined or randomised collection of practice questions.

CISSP CAT

Adapts question selection according to the examination algorithm and the candidate's responses.

Use mock exams to prepare — not to predict the real exam

The purpose is to develop knowledge, judgement, pacing and confidence.

📊 What Mock Exam Score means "Ready"? There is no universal magic percentage

Different practice-question providers have different question difficulty, scoring systems and test designs.

A score of 80% on one platform therefore cannot automatically be compared with 80% on another.

Look for consistency rather than one magic score

More useful signs of readiness include:

  • consistently strong performance across different question banks;
  • no major weak CISSP domain;
  • understanding why incorrect answers are incorrect;
  • being able to explain concepts rather than merely recognise answers;
  • comfortable pacing during long question sessions;
  • ability to answer unfamiliar scenario-based questions.
❌ Your Wrong Answers are the Valuable Ones Don't just chase a higher percentage
❌ Wrong Answer → Why did I choose it?
✅ Correct Answer → Why is this better?
🧠 Concept → What knowledge was I missing?
📚 Study → Return to the underlying topic
🔁 Retest → Can I answer a DIFFERENT question on the concept?
Do not memorise the letter

Memorising that "Question 73 = answer C" teaches almost nothing.

Understanding why C was the best answer teaches the underlying CISSP principle.

🔎 Classify your Mistakes Not every wrong answer has the same cause
Knowledge Gap

You genuinely did not know the concept.

Misread Question

You knew the concept but misunderstood what was being asked.

Technical Bias

You selected the most technical answer when a governance or risk-based response was more appropriate.

Sequence Error

Your answer was reasonable but was not the FIRST action required.

Overthinking

You invented additional assumptions not present in the scenario.

Changed Correct Answer

You talked yourself away from the most appropriate initial choice.

Different errors need different fixes

A knowledge gap requires study. A question-reading problem requires exam technique.

Key takeaways

Practice questions are a learning and assessment tool, not a substitute for understanding the CISSP curriculum.

Use diagnostic questions early to establish your starting point.

Use domain-specific quizzes while learning individual CISSP domains.

Use mixed-domain questions once you have covered most of the curriculum.

Use longer timed mock exams closer to the real examination.

Review incorrect answers carefully rather than focusing only on your percentage score.

Try to understand why the correct answer is best and why the alternatives are less appropriate.

Repeating the same questions can eventually measure memory rather than understanding.

Using more than one reputable question source can expose you to different scenario wording and styles.

There is no universal practice-test percentage that guarantees a CISSP pass.

Performance should be considered alongside conceptual understanding, consistency across domains and your ability to answer unfamiliar questions.

The real CISSP examination uses Computerized Adaptive Testing, so a conventional mock examination cannot reproduce the live exam exactly.

Mock exams remain useful for developing concentration, pacing and decision-making under pressure.

Avoid websites claiming to provide actual or leaked CISSP exam questions.

Actual examination material is confidential under the ISC2 examination agreement.

Most importantly: don't learn the question — learn the security principle behind the answer.
